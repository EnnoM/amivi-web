Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 20 October 2026

These standards apply to the app “Amivi: Friends & Plans” (Amivi). Amivi has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Minimum age

Amivi is only for people aged 16 and over, as set out in our Terms of Service. Accounts that we find to belong to someone under 16 are removed.

What is prohibited

Users must not use Amivi to create, share, request or link to any content or behaviour that sexually exploits or endangers children, including:

  • child sexual abuse material of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated material;
  • grooming, meaning contacting a minor to build trust for sexual purposes;
  • sexualising minors in messages, photos, voice messages, profiles or activities;
  • sextortion, meaning threatening to share intimate images of a minor;
  • trafficking or any other exploitation of minors.

How to report a concern in the app

  1. Open the profile of the user concerned.
  2. Open the menu and tap Report.
  3. Choose a reason (for example Inappropriate content), describe the concern and send the report. Reports are anonymous.

You can also block the user from their profile. Concerns about a specific message, photo or voice message can be sent to support@amivi.net with the subject “Child Safety”.

Please do not forward, save or screenshot abusive material yourself. Tell us where it is (the user and the chat), and we will secure it. If a child is in immediate danger, call the police right away (110 in Germany, 112 across the EU).

What we do

  • We aim to review every report within 24 hours.
  • We remove CSAM and any content that sexually exploits or endangers children as soon as we know about it.
  • We permanently ban the accounts responsible.
  • We report CSAM and cases of child sexual exploitation to the competent law enforcement authorities in Germany, and we cooperate with their requests.

Compliance with the law

Amivi complies with applicable child safety laws, including the German Criminal Code and the EU Digital Services Act.

Child safety contact

Questions about these standards or about how Amivi prevents child sexual abuse can be sent to support@amivi.net. For the provider's full details, see the Legal Notice.
